# Risk Profiling Engine - User Guide

## 🎯 What is Risk Profiling?

Risk profiling is the process of determining your investment risk capacity and tolerance. Our system automatically calculates your risk profile based on your financial information to provide personalized investment recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Create Your Financial Profile
1. Login to your account
2. Click on **"Profile"** in the navigation bar
3. Fill in your financial information:
   - Age
   - Monthly Income
   - Monthly Savings
   - Investment Horizon (how long you plan to invest)
   - Risk Tolerance (your comfort with market fluctuations)
   - Financial Goal

### Step 2: Automatic Risk Calculation
After saving your financial profile:
- ✨ Your risk profile is **automatically calculated**
- 🔄 You'll be redirected to the Risk Assessment page
- 📊 You'll see your risk score and investor type

### Step 3: View Your Results
On the Risk Assessment page, you'll see:
- **Risk Score**: A number between 0-100
- **Investor Type**: Conservative, Moderate, or Aggressive
- **Visual Gauge**: Beautiful circular gauge showing your score
- **Detailed Explanation**: Why you received this score
- **Investment Recommendations**: How to allocate your money

## 🔍 What Factors Determine Your Risk Score?

Our algorithm considers 5 key factors with different weights:

### 1. Age (15% of total score)
**Why it matters**: Younger investors have more time to recover from market downturns.

- **18-30 years**: High score (90-100)
- **31-40 years**: Moderate-high (70-89)
- **41-50 years**: Moderate (50-69)
- **51-60 years**: Moderate-low (30-49)
- **61+ years**: Low (0-29)

### 2. Monthly Income (10% of total score)
**Why it matters**: Higher income provides more financial cushion for risk-taking.

- **< ₹25,000**: Low score (20-30)
- **₹25,000 - ₹50,000**: Moderate-low (40-50)
- **₹50,000 - ₹75,000**: Moderate (55-65)
- **₹75,000 - ₹100,000**: Moderate-high (70-80)
- **> ₹100,000**: High (85-100)

### 3. Savings Rate (15% of total score)
**Why it matters**: Higher savings rate shows financial discipline and capacity.

- **< 10%**: Low (10-20)
- **10-20%**: Moderate-low (30-40)
- **20-30%**: Moderate (50-60)
- **30-40%**: Moderate-high (70-80)
- **> 40%**: High (85-100)

### 4. Investment Horizon (25% of total score)
**Why it matters**: Longer time horizons allow for recovery from short-term volatility.

- **1-3 Years**: Low (20)
- **3-5 Years**: Moderate-low (45)
- **5-10 Years**: Moderate-high (70)
- **10+ Years**: High (95)

### 5. Risk Tolerance (35% of total score) ⭐ **MOST IMPORTANT**
**Why it matters**: Your personal comfort level is the key factor.

- **Low**: Conservative (15)
- **Medium**: Moderate (50)
- **High**: Aggressive (90)

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my risk profile?
Yes! Update your financial profile anytime. Your risk score will recalculate based on new information.

### What if I disagree with my risk classification?
The risk profile is based on objective financial data and your stated preferences. If you believe it's incorrect:
1. Review your financial profile inputs
2. Adjust your risk tolerance setting
3. Consider if your self-assessment matches your financial capacity

### How often should I recalculate?
Recommended frequency:
- **Quarterly**: Quick check
- **Annually**: Full review
- **After major life events**: Immediate update

### Is a higher risk score better?
No! Higher isn't better or worse - it's about what's right for YOU. A conservative profile is perfect for someone near retirement, while an aggressive profile suits young investors.

### Can two people with same age have different scores?
Absolutely! Risk score depends on 5 factors, not just age. Income, savings, horizon, and tolerance all matter.

### What if I have no financial profile?
You need to create your financial profile first:
1. Click "Create Financial Profile" button
2. Fill in the form
3. Risk profile calculates automatically

### Is my data secure?
Yes! Your risk profile is:
- Tied to your account only
- Protected by authentication
- Not shared with anyone
- Private and confidential
